# Security policy

Report security vulnerabilities in Scope privately by email to
[hello@scopevcs.com](mailto:hello@scopevcs.com), with "Security" in the subject.
Do not open a public issue.

Include what you found, where, how to reproduce it, and the impact you expect.
You will get an acknowledgement within 5 days and updates as the report is
investigated. Once a fix ships, you can be credited if you want to be.

## Scope

- The Scope service at scopevcs.com and its API
- The Scope CLI
- The source code in this repository

Vulnerabilities in third-party services Scope uses, such as Clerk, Railway or
PostHog, should be reported to those providers.

## Safe harbor

Good-faith research that follows this policy is authorized, and Scope will not
pursue legal action over it. While testing:

- use only accounts and repositories you own or have permission to test;
- do not access, change or delete other people's data, and stop and report if
  you encounter it;
- do not degrade the service, for example with denial-of-service or load tests;
- give Scope reasonable time to fix the issue before disclosing it publicly.
